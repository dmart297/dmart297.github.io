Welcome!

Hello! My name is Drew Marting and I am a third-year Computer Science Major at California Polytechnic State University, San Luis Obispo. With a Computing for Interactive Arts Minor, I aim to combine my logical and creative sides, employing both in my pursuit of UX/UI.

On my college campus, I have been involved as an officer for Women Involved in Software and Hardware, a volunteer for the Cal Poly Cat Program, a dancer for United Movement, an instructor for teaching robotics at Cal Poly's local elementary school, an orientation leader for Week of Welcome, and more to do with school spirit.
