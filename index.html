Welcome!

Hello! My name is Drew Marting and I am a second-year Computer Science Major at California Polytechnic State University, San Luis Obispo. As a Computing for Interactive Arts Minor, I aim to combine my logical side with my creative, hoping to employ both in pursuing UX/UI.

On campus, I am an officer for Women Involved in Software and Hardware, a volunteer for the Cal Poly Cat Program, a dancer for United Movement, and an instructor volunteer for teaching robotics at Cal Poly's local elementary school.
